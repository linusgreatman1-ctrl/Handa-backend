FAQs
How do I book an artisan? ›
Select the service you need, describe your issue, set your location, and tap "Find Nearby Artisans". Verified artisans near you will be notified and can accept your request.
How does payment work? ›
Payment is made through FixMi only — never pay cash directly. After the job, you pay via bank transfer or card. FixMi holds the money and pays the artisan after your confirmation.
What if I'm not satisfied? ›
Raise a complaint within 24 hours. Our team reviews the case and can facilitate a refund or send another artisan at no extra cost.
How are artisans verified? ›
Every artisan must provide a government ID (NIN or Voter Card), a profile photo, and their phone number. Our admin team reviews each application before approval.
Can I call an artisan directly? ›
No — calling is disabled for your safety. All communication happens through FixMi's in-app chat only.

FAQs for Artisans
How do I receive payments? ›
After a customer confirms the job, FixMi releases 85% of the agreed amount to your registered bank account. Transfers are processed within 30 minutes for major banks.
What is FixMi's commission? ›
FixMi retains 15% of every job. For cash jobs, you must remit this commission within 7 days. Failure to do so will result in account suspension.
How do I get more job requests? ›
Stay online, maintain a high rating, respond quickly, and consider boosting your profile under Promote My Page. Artisans with 4.5+ ratings receive 3x more requests.
What if a customer is difficult? ›
Use the Report Customer button in your Job History. Provide details and our team will review within 24 hours. Never engage in arguments — complete the job or contact support.
Can I cancel a job? ›
Cancellations affect your rating. Only cancel via the app with a valid reason. Repeated cancellations may result in account suspension.
Safety & Trust
🛡️
Your Safety Matters
FixMi takes artisan safety seriously. These guidelines protect you on every job.
Before you arrive ›
Always verify the customer's address matches what's in the app before travelling. If anything seems suspicious, contact support before proceeding.
At the customer's location ›
Never enter a location that feels unsafe. Always keep your app open and active. If you feel threatened, leave immediately and contact support and the police.
Payments and money ›
Never accept payment outside the FixMi app unless the customer selects Cash as their payment method. If asked to use another payment platform, refuse and report it.
Reporting an incident ›
Use the Report Customer button in Job History or contact support directly. All reports are confidential and reviewed within 24 hours.
Emergency contacts ›
Police: 199 | FixMi Emergency: tap the Support button. Always trust your instincts — your safety comes first, before any job.

Safety & Trust
🛡️
Your Safety on FixMi
We protect every booking with these safety guidelines.
Never pay outside the app ›
Always pay through FixMi. Your money is held in escrow and released to the artisan only after you confirm the job is complete. Never hand cash directly or use another platform.
Verifying your artisan ›
Every artisan has submitted a government ID (NIN or Voter Card) verified by our admin team. Look for the ✓ Verified badge before booking.
Communication safety ›
All communication must happen through FixMi's in-app chat only. Calls are disabled for your protection. Never share personal contact details outside the app.
If you feel unsafe ›
End the job immediately. You will not be charged if cancelled before work begins. Contact FixMi support and authorities if necessary.
Emergency contacts ›
